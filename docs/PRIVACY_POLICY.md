# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 17, 2026

GloryPrep Learning ("we", "us", "our") operates the GloryPrep IELTS preparation Platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the Platform. Account registration captures your name, email address, and authentication identifier through our sign-in provider. Payment processing captures the billing details required by the payment gateway (PayPal); card data is handled exclusively by the gateway and never touches our servers. Usage data such as lesson progress, mock test attempts, and scores are stored to power your dashboard and results reports. Anonymous analytics help us understand which pages visitors use.

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Name, email, login method | Account creation, sign-in, unlock delivery |
| Payment data | SKU purchased, amount, transaction reference | Fulfilment, refunds, accounting |
| Progress data | Lesson completion, test scores, attempt history | Dashboard, results, mock test engine |
| Support data | Contact form messages | Customer support and moderation |
| Analytics | Page views, anonymous device category | Product improvement |

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to operate and improve the Platform, deliver purchased content to your account, respond to support requests, prevent fraud and abuse, and produce anonymous aggregate statistics. We do not sell personal information. Marketing communication is only sent where you have expressly opted in, and you may unsubscribe at any time.

## 3. Cookies and Local Storage

The Platform uses minimal cookies for session management, plus browser local storage to remember your lesson progress and preferences on your device. No advertising trackers or third-party marketing cookies are used.

## 4. Data Sharing

Your data may be shared with: (a) our hosting and database provider, solely to operate the Platform; (b) the PayPal payment gateway, to process payments; and (c) authorities where disclosure is required by law.

## 5. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. Progress data is retained to preserve your study history. Payment records are retained for the period required by tax and accounting law (typically seven years). Support messages are retained until resolution plus a reasonable archive period.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any right, email support@gloryprep.com. We will respond within thirty (30) days. You may also delete your account, which removes your personal profile; note that transaction records required by law may be kept in anonymised form.

## 7. Children

The Platform is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

We apply industry-standard security measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS), server-side session management, role-based access controls, and input validation. No system is entirely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. International Transfers

Our infrastructure operates in multiple jurisdictions. By using the Platform you consent to the transfer of your information to, and processing in, those locations, subject to the safeguards described here.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision; material changes will be highlighted on the Platform.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@gloryprep.com.
