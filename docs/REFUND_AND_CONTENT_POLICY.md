# Refund and Content Originality Policy

**Last updated:** August 17, 2026

This policy accompanies the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. It covers two areas: refunds for digital purchases and the originality of all practice content.

## 1. Refund Policy

GloryPrep sells digital educational products. Because digital content is delivered instantly and cannot be "returned," we offer a fair-use refund window designed to balance access with protection against misuse.

| Situation | Refund outcome |
|---|---|
| Request within 7 days of purchase, less than 10% of content accessed | Full refund |
| Request within 7 days, more than 10% accessed | No refund (content substantially consumed) |
| Request after 7 days | No refund |
| Duplicate or accidental charge | Full refund regardless of timing |
| Payment failed but money debited | Full refund after verification |
| Content error (broken file, corrupted material) | Full refund or re-delivery, your choice |

To request a refund, email support@gloryprep.com with the email address used at purchase and the transaction reference. Decisions are issued within five (5) working days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

## 2. Content Originality

Every practice question, passage, lesson, and mock examination on GloryPrep is authored originally by our team. We do not copy, paraphrase-for-copy, or reproduce material from Cambridge IELTS publications, IDP or British Council materials, Makkar IELTS, Barron's, The Official Cambridge Guide, or any other published resource. Content is written to match the style, format, and difficulty of public IELTS preparation conventions, which are not protected by copyright, but no third-party intellectual property is used. If you believe any item on the Platform resembles protected material, notify us at support@gloryprep.com and we will review and, if warranted, remove or rewrite the content.

## 3. Score Estimates

Mock test scores are honest estimates calculated from the official publicly published conversion tables. We do not inflate, fabricate, or "guarantee" scores. Band-score results on the Platform are indicators of preparation progress, not predictions of official exam outcomes.

## 4. Honest Marketing Commitment

GloryPrep does not use fabricated testimonials, inflated user counts, fake "recommended" badges, or misleading discount labels. All marketing statistics shown on the Platform are either real platform data or clearly labelled as illustrative examples (for instance, the example band-score card on the homepage).
